Escape to a different reality

Our Mini Adventure is a relaxing fantasy walking simulator. There is no rush and no map — just an enchanting world to wander at your own pace. Find something important that has been lost.

The world waiting for you beyond the portals

  • Cast spells to teleport through the portals — use the magic you learn to visit your favourite places
  • Build your spell book — gather new spells as you wander; you never know when they will be useful
  • Meet magical characters — a stranger is just a friend you haven't made yet
  • Learn to fly on a winged horse — unlock the freedom to soar above
  • Wander sun-drenched beaches — let the sound of lapping waves help you drift off
  • Drift through flower meadows — relax in expansive, colourful fields of beauty

An enchanting world to relax and lose yourself in

There's golden light in the forest at dawn. Listen to a calm river winding past weeping willows and mossy rocks. Pause and listen to the gentle pitter-patter of rain falling around you. Is that a wizard's tower silhouetted against a shimmering aurora? A bubbling cauldron, where you create your spells. And in the study, a brass orrery that changes the time of day and the seasons to your choice.

No rush, no map

Nothing here is chasing you. Stand waist deep in whispering grass, sit by a stream, watch a waterfall, follow a path simply because it looks inviting, or take to the skies and fly on a winged horse.

A gentle, enchanting original soundtrack by Gita Puspita accompanies you throughout.

Mindful Design

To support immersion and a continuous soothing mindful experience, conventional loading screens are replaced with magic portals that instantly transport you between your favourite places, as naturally as walking through a door.

Be careful you don’t lose track of time completely, forget which side of the portal you are from and where you left the real world.

The developer accepts no responsibility for hours lost beyond the portal, neglected chores, missed appointments, broken marriages, or arguments over who gets custody of the winged horse.
